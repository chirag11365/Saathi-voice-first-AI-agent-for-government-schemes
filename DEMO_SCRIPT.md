# Saathi demo script (2 minutes)

1. Open Saathi and select Hindi (or English, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu). Explain that the language choice is remembered.
2. Use the Farmer demo quick start. It jumps to a sample profile and shows matching schemes; alternatively answer the guided questions one at a time and skip anything.
3. Show the eligible-first scheme list, eligibility ring, short explanation, benefit, official source, date checked, and document checklist. Tick a document, filter by Eligible/Maybe, and try Explain simply or Listen.
4. Open Ask Saathi from the floating button or a scheme card. Ask about benefits or required documents. If Gemini is unavailable, the saved FAQ answers from scheme data still work. Toggle light/dark mode if useful for the audience.
5. Open the optional profile chat and enter a phone number or bank-like value. Saathi discards it and reminds the user that demo values will be used.
6. Select Apply for me on a matching scheme. The mock portal fills a fixed fake bank account and fake Aadhaar ending 1234 only in masked fields. Show the demo privacy notice and four-step progress, review screen, then confirm.
7. Finish with the tracking ID, copy/share controls, spoken summary, and downloadable demo receipt. Clarify that the eligibility rules and portal are demonstrations, not a government decision.

Never enter or show a real person's name, phone, email, Aadhaar, bank account, or identity document during the demo.
